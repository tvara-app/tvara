Yes — and for “Tvara = an extension”, there are real TLDs that contain pieces of extension.

The important distinction is that the TLD itself doesn't have to literally mean “extension”.

Best real TLDs I found

DomainWhy it works
.expressreads as “extension” visually
.exchangestarts with ex
.expertstarts with ex

Unfortunately .ex isn't currently a delegated public TLD.

My shortlist

  1. tvara.express — the strongest of them
  2. tvara.tech — plain, and nobody misreads it
  3. tvara.tools — accurate about what this is
  4. tvara.run — short, and it says motion

If the requirement is that the TLD literally be a piece of EXTENSION, public DNS is much more restrictive than that.
