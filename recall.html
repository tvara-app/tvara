Your archive

Chats are archived automatically as you open them. The archive lives in this browser's local extension storage — it never touches any server, because this extension has no network permissions.

Get your full history in

  1. ChatGPT — automatic: open chatgpt.com, press ⌘⇧K and hit Sync full ChatGPT history. Every chat's title, dates and text sync straight from your own account into the local archive — after the first run it keeps itself up to date.
  2. Claude — import file: Settings → Privacy → Export data, then drop the file below.
  3. Gemini, DeepSeek, Grok, Perplexity: no export or usable history API — chats are archived automatically as you open them.
