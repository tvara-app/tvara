Your archive

Chats are archived automatically as you open them. The archive lives in this browser's local extension storage — it never touches any server, because this extension has no network permissions.

Sync your full history

Pulls every conversation — titles, dates and text — straight from your own account into this local archive. It reads only the same data the app itself loads, and nothing leaves your machine. Big histories take a while; you can close this and it keeps going in that app's tab.

ChatGPT and Claude sync automatically. Gemini, Grok, DeepSeek and Perplexity have no history API, so their chats are archived as you open them — or import a file below.
