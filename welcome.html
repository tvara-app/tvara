What happens on its own

  • Long chats stop lagging. Messages you can't see are put to sleep and wake instantly when you scroll back. Nothing is deleted, ever. Free on every platform, forever.
  • Your chats get archived, locally. The archive builds itself in the background so Total Recall has something to search. It lives in this browser and has nowhere else to go — there is no Long Chat Toolkit server.
  • A minimap, an outline, timestamps and stars appear on the chats themselves. Hover the right edge — the same strip holds Continue in a new chat, which carries this conversation's context into a fresh one when it gets long or slow.
  • You get told before you hit a limit. At 20% of your allowance, and again at 10%, on whichever AI is running down — instead of finding out when it cuts you off.
