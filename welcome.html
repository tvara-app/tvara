What it does on its own

  • Long chats stop lagging. Messages you can't see sleep, and wake the moment you scroll back. Nothing is ever deleted.
  • Your chats archive themselves, locally. Conversation text never leaves this browser — not to us, not to anyone. (Licensing talks to a small server of ours; it never sees a conversation. Details.)
  • A minimap, outline, timestamps and stars appear on the chat itself. Hover the right edge.
  • Continue in a new chat. When one gets long or slow, carry its context into a fresh one.
  • A warning before you run out. At 20% of your allowance, then 10%, instead of when it cuts you off.
