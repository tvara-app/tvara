What Tvara does

Speed engine

Reduces rendering work for off-screen messages in long loaded chats. It does not delete or alter provider messages.

Minimap and in-chat search

Navigate and search the conversation open in the current tab.

Local archive search

Search chats previously opened on supported AI sites. The archive stays in extension storage on your device.

Context Bridge and backup

Add selected local context to a prompt, export chats, and create encrypted backup files in Downloads.

Tvara supports ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, DeepSeek, and Grok. Their interfaces can change, which can temporarily affect a provider-specific feature.

Public launch support: current Chrome Stable and Beta on Windows, macOS, Linux, and ChromeOS, plus current Edge on Windows and macOS. Mobile Chrome, Firefox, Safari, and unsupported browser versions are not supported for this launch.

Privacy Policy

Effective: 10 September 2026

Chat content stays on your device

Tvara reads chats from supported AI sites only to provide local speed, navigation, search, archive, backup, context-handoff, and usage-tracking features. It does not send chat text, titles, prompts, exports, or chat URLs to Tvara or another third party. Tvara does not use behavioural analytics, tracking pixels, advertising identifiers, or third-party SDKs.

Services Tvara connects to

  • Supported AI sites: Tvara reads conversations you open on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, DeepSeek, and Grok, using your existing browser session. The chat data is stored locally.
  • Google: after you choose Google sign-in for trial, purchase, restore, or device management, Tvara requests only OpenID and email identity information.
  • Licence issuer: Tvara uses a Cloudflare Worker to issue trials, validate purchases, restore purchases, enforce device limits, process refunds, and revoke entitlements. It receives entitlement and device proof, not chat content.
  • Dodo Payments: our merchant of record processes checkout and refunds under its own privacy terms.

What the licence issuer stores

The issuer stores the minimum records needed for account identity, trial eligibility, licence ownership, device limits, fraud prevention, refunds, and support: identity and email fingerprints, an encrypted signed-in email address, licence and device identifiers or hashes, purchase and webhook references, trial timestamps, revocation state, and short-lived security and rate-limit records. It retains identity and entitlement records for up to 400 days of inactivity and longer where a live licence, legal obligation, refund, fraud-prevention need, or support request requires it. It retains hashed IP abuse signals for up to 30 days.

Permissions

  • storage and unlimitedStorage keep settings and the archive locally.
  • downloads writes exports and encrypted backup files to Downloads.
  • alarms schedules local archive, backup, entitlement, and session checks across service-worker suspension.
  • notifications displays local deletion-review, sign-out, and allowance notices.
  • identity opens Google sign-in only after you choose it and requests openid email only.
  • Host access is limited to supported AI sites for automatic local features and Tvara’s own post-purchase page.

Tvara does not request browser history, tabs, cookies, bookmarks, web requests, scripting, or access to sites outside its named host permissions.

Your choices

You can remove local archive data from the extension and remove backup files from your device. For access, deletion, support, or refund requests, email tvara.exten@gmail.com. Deleting an identity or licence record can release device slots and prevent restoration.
