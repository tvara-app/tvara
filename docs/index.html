What it does

⚡Speed engine

Messages outside your view are put to sleep and wake instantly as you scroll. A 2,000-message chat feels like a 20-message chat. Watch the 💤 counter work.

➡️Continue in a new chat

When a chat gets long, slow, or hits a limit: one click opens a fresh one with the context already in the prompt box: your original ask, your starred messages, the latest code. Quoted, never summarised. Nothing is sent for you.

⏳Told before the wall

These apps warn you by cutting you off. This warns you at 20% and again at 10%, on whichever AI is running down, from the provider's own figure.

🕒Message timestamps

AI chat sites don't show when anything was said. Hover any message to see its time: real send times on ChatGPT, honest "first seen" times elsewhere.

🗺️Minimap

A VS Code-style strip of the whole conversation. Hover for previews, click to jump anywhere.

🔎In-chat search

Instant full-text search with a match counter and one-key jumping, even across sleeping messages (⌘⇧F / Ctrl+Shift+F).

⤵Resume

Reopen a long chat and one tap returns you to the exact message you were reading.

💾One-click backup

Export the conversation to clean Markdown or JSON, with structure, code blocks and timestamps preserved. Your chats, on your disk.

Instant licence key by email. 5 devices. No subscription.

Payments are processed by our merchant of record. After purchase you receive a license key by email; paste it into the extension popup to activate. Activating registers that device with the licence server, and it sends your key and a device label such as “Chrome · macOS”, never a cookie and never any conversation text. After that a licensed copy re-checks at most once a month, and it never withdraws Pro because a check failed to get through. See devices.

Devices

One Pro licence activates on 5 devices.

  • A “device” is a signed-in browser profile. Your laptop and desktop on the same Chrome profile share one slot; Firefox, Edge, or a second profile each take their own.
  • Open the extension popup and click Devices to see them. Release frees the one you're on; Terminate frees any other.
  • If every slot is full when you activate, the extension frees your oldest device automatically and carries on. It only stops to ask when the slots are held by devices this browser has never seen.
  • Clicking Remove in the popup hands the slot back, so do that before wiping a machine.
  • Lost a device without releasing it, or stuck at the limit? Email tvara.exten@gmail.com from your purchase address and we'll clear every slot on your licence, no questions.

Licence keys issued before this change (they start LCT1.) are unaffected: they verify offline on your own machine and have no device limit.

Privacy Policy

Effective: July 2026

Tvara is built so that trust is verifiable, not promised:

  • The extension collects no data. Your conversations, settings, reading positions, local timestamp records and license key are stored in your browser's local extension storage. An explicit Total Recall history check contacts only the supported AI provider endpoints listed in the manifest; there is no Tvara server, analytics endpoint or chat-data upload.
  • No accounts, no analytics, no tracking, in the extension and on this website. This site is a static page with no cookies and no third-party scripts.
  • On ChatGPT only, a small read-only script runs in the page to read each message's timestamp from the app's own state. It makes no network requests and changes nothing on the page. All processing is local.
  • Purchases are handled by our payment provider (merchant of record). We receive your email address solely to issue your license key. We do not receive or store your card details.
  • Licence activation is the one request that isn't to an AI site. Activating Pro sends your licence key and a coarse device label to the payment provider's licence server so it can enforce the 5-device limit; a licensed copy re-checks there at most once a month. That is the entire payload: no cookies, no conversation text, no identifiers beyond a random per-profile ID we generate. The free tier never contacts it, and the extension requests no additional host permission to do it.
  • The extension ships as unminified, unobfuscated JavaScript. Everything running on your machine is in the extension folder, readable, exactly as we wrote it and exactly as Chrome reviewed it.
